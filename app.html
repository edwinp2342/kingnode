Start here

Kingnode reads the options chain the way a dealer desk does and shows you where price gets pinned and where it accelerates.

  1. Pick a ticker (top bar or the quick list). The chart moves with the tape — every 20 seconds for the core names — and shows the walls, flip and expected move; the right rail reads them for you. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click to drop a line, F for full screen.
  2. Map → Heatseeker: the grid is strike × expiration, the ladder on the right says buy / sell / don't fade per level. ★ is the king node.
  3. Trade → Playbook: your rules (risk %, stop %, DTE) and your positions. Desk → Journal logs every trade with the chain attached and coaches you from the data.
  4. Hide anything you don't use with Customize. Turn explanations off with Help once you know the screens.

Keys: / search · R refresh · F chart full screen · A Ava · [ ] switch tabs · H help · Esc closes the inspector or full screen.

Data is 15-minute delayed on the free feed. Ideas and the Quant desk are labeled LABS: useful, measured where possible, not proven. Nothing here is advice.
