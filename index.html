What you get

Everything the expensive terminals show. Explained, and labelled.

Most positioning tools hand you a wall of colors and a Discord. Kingnode tells you what the colors mean, adds the parts they charge extra for, and never lets a stale number look fresh.

HEATSEEKER

Dealer gamma by strike and expiration

Teal pins, coral accelerates. The biggest nodes are flagged so you're not squinting for them, and a history of snapshots shows positioning migrate during the day.

LEVELS

Call wall, put wall, gamma flip, max pain

The four numbers most traders actually use, at the top of the screen, drawn on the chart, and tracked in a levels history so you can see when a wall moved.

THE READ

A regime call in plain English

"Positive gamma, spot 0.4% above the flip, call wall at 7,800" — written as a paragraph you can act on, with the caveats attached instead of hidden.

EXPECTED MOVE

Straddle-implied range, with a scorecard

0DTE through LEAPS with ATM IV and the ± range — and a running record of how often the market actually stayed inside the band, so you know how much to trust it.

FRESH POSITIONING

Volume against open interest

Contracts trading 1.5× their OI today — new bets ranked by dollar notional — instead of a firehose of every print.

FLOW

What changed, contract by contract

Session flow derived from chain changes, the most active contracts, dealer-side read, FINRA short volume and insider filings, one click from the heatmap. Labelled as derived, because it is.

CHARTING

A chart that keeps up — in every session

Candles that move with the tape, pre-market, regular, after hours and the overnight 24-hour print; walls, flip and the expected-move band on price; EMAs, VWAP, Bollinger, RSI, prior-day levels, opening range; one minute to daily; full screen with F; your own lines.

THE LAB

Backtests and probabilities on ten years of data, in your browser

Say what you would buy, when, and when you would sell; the engine runs it the honest way — next-open fills, slippage, stops before targets — and then tells you how much to believe it: Wilson intervals next to the base rate, walk-forward out-of-sample, Monte Carlo drawdowns, and a count of how many variants you have tried. A research journal remembers every run and reruns it on today's data.

THE CLOSING READ

3:50 PM: which way the bell leans, graded

A notification ten minutes before the close — Buy-side · grade B (+47) — built from the last half hour of signed volume in the core names, the market-wide sweeps, SPY/QQQ/IWM against VWAP, breadth, volume pace and SPX gamma. Scored after the bell against SPY's actual move, grade by grade, so you can see what the grades are worth.

SUSPICIOUS FLOW

Abnormal money, graded against the name's own baseline

Premium surges, blocks with the aggressor side, one-sided days and contracts trading many times their open interest — each scored A/B/C against that name's last 20 sessions, paced for the time of day. New A grades notify you; Ava writes the flow update.

BREADTH

The whole market, not just your ticker

Advancers and decliners, up- and down-volume, names above their 20- and 50-day, new highs and lows — swept across 5,000+ optionable stocks every five minutes, with an intraday breadth line.

ANY TICKER

Search every stock, ETF and index

Type a ticker or a company name, or hit ⌘K and type anything: tickers, tabs, chart intervals, actions. Hundreds of names are pre-computed; everything else loads on demand.

CALENDAR + MACRO

The releases that move it, and the latest readings

Verified BLS, BEA and FOMC dates through 2027, opex and witching, your own events with notifications — and the latest CPI, jobs, fed funds and Treasury yields, fetched hourly from the publishers.

JOURNAL + IDEAS

Your trades, graded honestly

A journal with coach stats — drawdown, outlier dependence, loss tails, session breakdown — and an Ideas card whose backtest shows costs, gross versus net and an out-of-sample walk-forward, even when the answer is unflattering.

Price and level alerts to the browser or Discord, a halal screen for the universe, one-click backups that never contain a key, and a layout you arrange yourself. Installs to a phone home screen.

Ava · the desk assistant

Ask the desk. She reads the same screen you do, and only that.

Ava is Claude, given Kingnode's data as tools instead of a memory of the market. She answers from the chain, the flow, the chart, the calendar and your journal — and says which, and how old.

What she can do

  • Read the setup — levels, regime, expected move, fresh positioning for any ticker, with the data's age attached.
  • Scan the market — the whole universe by premium, unusual volume, breadth; pull price history; score an idea against the rules.
  • Work the journal — log and close trades, grade them, keep notes you tell her to remember.
  • Check the plumbing — a data_status tool tells her (and you) whether a number is live, delayed or stale before she quotes it.
  • Know the calendar — verified release dates and the latest official readings, periods included.
  • Run the Lab — she turns "buy when it drops 3% in an uptrend, sell at +5%" into a spec, backtests it, reads the probability study, and reports the validation lines before the returns.
  • Call the close and the flow — closing_read gives her the 3:50 lean with its record; flow_alerts the graded anomalies, new first. She posts a flow update on her own when new A grades land.

Guardrails

  • Your own Anthropic key, stored in your browser only. It never touches a server or a backup.
  • Every write — a trade logged, a trade closed, a memory — shows you the exact payload and waits for Allow.
  • An audit log of every tool call, a rate limit, and a guard against instructions smuggled in through data.
  • She never says "real-time" unless the source is; "no reading" beats a guess.
  • No orders. Ever.

FAQ

Questions traders ask.

Is it real-time?

On this site, no, and it says so: quotes are a snapshot about 20–45 seconds behind the tape and chains are Cboe's 15-minute-delayed ones. That is plenty for levels that move on open interest, which updates once a day. Real-time comes two ways — your own Robinhood connection inside Claude's preview (quotes, bid × ask and a full chain, with your consent), or the included streaming server with Alpaca or Polygon keys. Each is labelled as what it is: IEX is one exchange, not the consolidated tape, and the badge never pretends otherwise.

How is dealer gamma calculated?

Gamma × open interest × 100 × spot² × 1%, per strike and expiration. The default model assumes dealers are long calls and short puts, the standard convention; a magnitude-only model is one click away. The heatmap, walls and flip all derive from that, and the levels history shows how they moved.

Does this tell me what to trade?

No. It tells you where hedging flows are likely to support, cap or accelerate price so you can plan around them. Positioning is context, not a signal, and nothing here is investment advice. The Ideas card ranks setups against written rules and shows you a backtest that is allowed to be unflattering.

Where do my keys live?

In your browser's storage, nowhere else. The Anthropic key for Ava, a GitHub token for restarting the feed and any proxy URL are stripped from backups and never sent to a server Kingnode runs. The streaming server, if you run one, keeps its provider keys in its own environment; the page only ever sends it symbol names.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. It is one HTML file and a feed that runs on GitHub Actions. npm run serve runs the same app locally with the streaming tier; there is a desktop build as well. The source is public.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is a personal build. Ava uses your own Anthropic key at Anthropic's prices; real-time data, if you want it, is whatever your provider charges — the terminal itself adds nothing.
