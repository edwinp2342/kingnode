Questions traders ask.

Where does the data come from, and is it real-time?

Options chains with open interest, volume, IV and greeks, refreshed every minute and delayed 15 minutes. That's plenty for levels that move on open interest, which updates once a day. Real-time flow is on the roadmap for Pro.

How is dealer gamma calculated?

Gamma × open interest × 100 × spot² × 1%, per strike and expiration. Default model assumes dealers are long calls and short puts (the standard convention); you can flip to a magnitude-only model in the controls. The heatmap, walls and flip all derive from that.

Does this tell me what to trade?

No. It tells you where hedging flows are likely to support, cap or accelerate price so you can plan around them. Positioning is context, not a signal, and nothing here is investment advice.
